faqs:
  - question: "Which wash level should I choose?"
    answer: "Level 1 keeps a clean car clean, so it suits regular upkeep between bigger jobs. Level 2 is the one most people book: the car gets properly cleaned inside and out, including the engine bay and the wheel arches, and you leave with two to three months of protection on the paint. Choose Level 3 when the paint feels rough or gritty after a wash. That roughness is dirt that has bonded to the surface, and it needs chemicals to lift rather than more soap."
  - question: "Why does an SUV cost more than a sedan?"
    answer: "It is a bigger car, so every step takes longer. More paint to wash, bigger wheels and arches, and more interior to vacuum. The difference is AED 20 on Level 1, AED 10 on Level 2, and AED 25 on Level 3. If you are not sure which applies to your car, send us the model and we will confirm before you book."
  - question: "How long does the protection last?"
    answer: "About two to three months on Level 2, and about six months on Level 3. It adds shine and keeps the car cleaner between washes, so dirt rinses off more easily. It is not the same as a full ceramic coating, which is a separate service and lasts years. If your car already has a coating, this tops it up rather than replacing it."
  - question: "How often should I wash my car in Dubai?"
    answer: "Every one to two weeks, and more often when there is a sandstorm. Dubai dust is gritty rather than soft, so if you leave it sitting on the paint it gets rubbed in the next time anything touches the car, including your own hand. Washing regularly is the cheapest way to protect the paint you already have."
  - question: "Is hand washing really better than an automatic wash?"
    answer: "Yes. An automatic wash runs the same brushes over your car that it ran over every car before yours, and that is what creates the fine circular scratches you see in sunlight. We rinse and foam the car first so the grit lifts off before anything touches the paint, then wash by hand with clean microfiber. On a dark car the difference shows within a few washes."
  - question: "Can you get rid of water spots, tar and bird droppings?"
    answer: "Yes, and that is what Level 3 is for. It uses stronger washes plus dedicated tar and iron removal to lift the things normal soap leaves behind. Most water spots come off at that stage. If a spot has already etched into the clear coat, washing will not fix it and the car needs paint correction instead. We will look at it and tell you honestly which one it is."
