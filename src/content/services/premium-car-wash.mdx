faqs:
  - question: "What is the difference between the three wash levels?"
    answer: "Method, not time. Level 1 is maintenance: interior vacuum and wipe down, underbody wash, a non-alkaline pre-wash, contact wash, basic trim restore and glass. Level 2 adds an alkaline pre-wash, the full rims, tyres and arches, engine bay deep cleaning, crevice and panel detailing with brushes, and a ceramic sealant. Level 3 adds chemical decontamination on top: acidic wash, neutralising wash, tar removal, iron removal, carpet extraction, engine bay protection, and a longer-lasting ceramic. If your paint feels rough to the touch after washing, that is bonded contamination and you want Level 3."
  - question: "Why do sedans and SUVs have different prices?"
    answer: "Surface area and time. An SUV has more panel, larger wheels and arches, and more interior to vacuum and wipe, so every stage takes longer. The gap is AED 20 on Level 1, AED 10 on Level 2, and AED 25 on Level 3. If you are unsure which rate applies to your vehicle, send us the model and we will confirm before you book."
  - question: "How long does the ceramic protection last?"
    answer: "Level 2 applies a ceramic sealant that lasts around two to three months. Level 3 applies an advanced ceramic that lasts around six months. Both are wash protection rather than a full ceramic coating: they add gloss and make the car easier to clean, but they are not a substitute for a professionally applied coating, which lasts years. If you already have a ceramic coating, these top it up rather than replace it."
  - question: "How often should I wash my car in Dubai?"
    answer: "Every one to two weeks in normal conditions, and more often during sandstorm season. Dubai's dust is abrasive, and letting it sit means it gets ground into the paint the next time anything touches the car. Regular washing also preserves whatever protection is already on the paint, whether that is a ceramic coating, paint protection film, or the ceramic applied during a Level 2 or Level 3 wash."
  - question: "Is hand washing really better than an automatic car wash?"
    answer: "Yes, and the reason is the brushes. Automatic washes drag the same bristles across every car that came before yours, which is how swirl marks appear. We pre-rinse and pre-wash first so grit is lifted before anything makes contact, then wash with dedicated cleaned microfiber. On a dark or freshly corrected car the difference shows within a few washes."
  - question: "Can you remove water spots, tar and bird droppings?"
    answer: "Yes, and that is what Level 3 is for. It uses an acidic wash followed by a neutralising wash, plus dedicated tar and iron removal, which lifts bonded contamination that a normal wash leaves behind. Light water spots usually come off during that stage. Spots that have already etched into the clear coat need paint correction rather than washing, and we will tell you honestly which one you are looking at."
