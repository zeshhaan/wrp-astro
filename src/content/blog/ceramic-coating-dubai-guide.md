Ceramic coating is useful if you want dust and road grime to release more easily when you wash your car. If your main concern is chips on the bonnet from motorway driving, look at [paint protection film](/services/paint-protection-film/). A coating leaves a thin layer on the paint. Film gives a stone something thicker to strike.

## What does ceramic coating cost at WRP?

WRP’s [ceramic coating service](/services/ceramic-coating/) starts at **AED 1,399**. The final quote depends on the size of your car, the condition of its paint, the coating selected and the surfaces you want covered. Ask for the paintwork, wheels, trim and glass to be listed separately so you can compare two quotes on the same basis.

Two cars of the same model can need different preparation. Look at the bonnet in direct light. If the reflection shows fine circular marks from washing, those marks will still be visible under a coating unless the paint is polished first. WRP assesses the finish before quoting for correction. Our [polish and paint correction guide](/blog/car-polish-vs-paint-correction-guide/) explains what can be improved and when to leave a deeper scratch alone.

## What happens between drop-off and collection?

The studio washes and decontaminates the paint, inspects the panels and carries out the agreed polishing work. The installer then removes polishing residue, applies the coating and lets it cure before inspecting the finish. WRP lists **24–48 hours for curing and inspection**. Allow additional time for preparation. Ask for the collection time once the studio has seen the car.

At handover, record the product name, the surfaces treated, the date of the first permitted wash and any warranty maintenance requirements. This matters especially if the bumper has film and the rest of the car has a coating. The two products can have different care instructions.

## How does a coated car cope with Dubai dust and water spots?

Water beads on a coated panel, but each bead can still leave a mineral ring when it dries. After the initial curing period, wash in the shade on cool panels. Rinse loose dust away first, wash with a coating-safe shampoo and dry the paint with a clean, soft towel. Wiping dust off a dry, dark bonnet can leave marks even when it is coated.

If sprinkler water dries on the car, start with a gentle wash. If a ring remains, ask the installer to inspect it before using an abrasive polish that could remove the coating. For a newly treated car, use the [first-month aftercare guide](/blog/first-30-days-after-ppf-window-tint-ceramic-coating/) alongside the instructions for your product.

## How long should the coating last?

WRP gives a typical range of three to five years, depending on the product, preparation and maintenance. Ask which term and conditions apply to your quote. Beading alone is a poor way to judge the coating’s remaining life. Traffic film or mineral deposits can change how water runs off one panel while the rest of the car still beads.
